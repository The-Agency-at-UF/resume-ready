# User-provided resume bullet examples

Use these examples to learn the user's preferred structure: direct action, concrete scope, and a clear result or purpose. They are style references, not evidence about a new project. Some are longer than the default 15–25 word target; select the strongest details rather than copying their length or every clause.

Retain the skill's accuracy rules when adapting them. Claims about engagement, inquiries, donations, savings, patents, or other outcomes require supporting documents even when written with X placeholders. If unverified, recast the outcome as an intended benefit. Do not infer causation from a class average, audience size, or competitor comparison.
